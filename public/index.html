Finds Sky Q, Sky Q Mini and Sky+HD boxes on this network.
Demo: four sample Sky boxes. Keys are shown, not sent. On site, use the Android app or the Pi bridge.

Boxes on the network

Add by IP or scan another subnet

The scan checks the subnets the bridge (this computer or the site Pi) is on. Sky boxes on a separate VLAN need that subnet entered here, and the network must route to it.

Pick a box on the left, or press Find boxes.

Keyboard: arrows, Enter OK, Backspace back, 0-9, P power, H home, Space play/pause
